# Supply Chain Analysis Project

A data analytics project focused on analyzing supply chain operations, sales performance, manufacturing costs, and shipping efficiency to uncover business insights and identify opportunities for operational improvement.

## Dataset Overview

* **Dataset Size:** 100+ rows and 24 columns.
* **Data Coverage:** Product categories, pricing, sales, manufacturing, suppliers, shipping, and operational costs.
* **Business Objective:** Evaluate supply chain performance, identify inefficiencies, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Setup and Requirements

Install the required Python libraries:

```bash
pip install pandas numpy seaborn matplotlib
```

**Getting Started:**

1. Place `supply_chain_data.csv` in the project directory.
2. Install the required dependencies.
3. Open and execute the Jupyter Notebook to explore the analysis.

## Analysis Components

### Python Data Analysis

* Performed data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis.
* Conducted statistical analysis to identify trends and patterns.
* Analyzed sales performance, manufacturing operations, and shipping efficiency.
* Created visualizations to communicate key findings.

### SQL Analysis

* Analyzed revenue distribution across product categories.
* Evaluated supplier performance and operational efficiency.
* Examined shipping costs and transportation methods.

### Business Intelligence Dashboards

* **Excel:** Developed summary visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).
* **Power BI:** Created an interactive dashboard to explore supply chain metrics and business performance.

## Key Findings

### Sales Performance

* **Product Categories:** Skincare accounted for approximately 45% of the business, based on the analysis.
* **Customer Preferences:** Identified differences in product preferences across customer segments.
* **Regional Performance:** Examined sales distribution across locations, including Kolkata and Delhi.

### Operational Analysis

* **Product Quality:** Identified variations in defect rates across product categories, with haircare showing a reported defect rate of 37%.
* **Lead Times:** Analyzed differences in delivery lead times across product types.
* **Manufacturing Costs:** Compared production costs across categories, identifying skincare as a significant cost area.

### Shipping and Logistics

* **Carrier Performance:** Compared shipping costs, delivery speed, and revenue-related performance across carriers.
* **Transportation Analysis:** Evaluated road, air, and other shipping methods for cost and operational efficiency.
* **Cost Optimization:** Identified opportunities to improve shipping decisions by balancing delivery speed and transportation expenses.
